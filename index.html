KEPTA

Your AI assistant forgets you after every chat. KEPTA remembers — on your own computer, in one encrypted file.

Not a notes app. A memory your AI reads.

What KEPTA does

Encrypted at restYour entire memory lives in one SQLCipher-encrypted database. The key never leaves your machine's keychain.
Hybrid retrievalBM25 full-text + vector search + knowledge graph, fused and re-ranked locally. Relevant answers, not keyword soup.
Works with every AIKEPTA ships an MCP server — Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor, Windsurf, VS Code, Gemini CLI, Cline, Roo Code and any MCP client can read and write your memory.
Private by designPrivate notes never leave the app — they are invisible even to connected AI clients. No telemetry, no cloud, no account.
